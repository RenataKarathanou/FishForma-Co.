About FishForma Co.

Our Mission

To deliver science-based consulting and innovative solutions for sustainable fisheries management and Blue Economy development, combining technical expertise with stakeholder engagement.

Our Vision

A thriving Blue Economy where fisheries are managed sustainably, communities benefit equitably, and marine ecosystems thrive through evidence-based decision-making.

Our Founder

Eirini Renata Karathanou - Ichthyologist with extensive experience in fisheries research, environmental management, and international project development in sustainable aquatic resource governance.

Our Services

Project Development

Design and implementation of integrated fisheries and aquaculture projects with focus on sustainability and stakeholder benefit.

Technical Advisory

Expert guidance on fisheries management, environmental regulations, and best practices for sustainable operations.

Field Research

Comprehensive data collection and analysis for stock assessment, ecosystem monitoring, and resource evaluation.

Business Planning

Strategic development and feasibility studies for fisheries enterprises and Blue Economy initiatives.

Stakeholder Engagement

Facilitation of inclusive dialogue and capacity building among fishing communities, government, and private sector.

Education & Training

Specialized workshops and training programs for fisheries professionals and community members.
